# 📬 Email Dash

**Because email is terrible: a Skinner’s box for your inbox.**

Email Dash is a Chrome extension that transforms Gmail into a fast-paced productivity game. Designed to help you process emails faster while having a little fun, it turns your inbox chaos into points, streaks, and progress. Archive, reply, and power through your inbox to level up and earn rewards.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🎮 **Gamified Inbox**: Earn points for archiving, deleting, and sending emails.
- ⏱️ **Timer Mechanics**: Beat the clock and get rewarded for your speed.
- 🔁 **Streak Tracking**: Keep up your email-processing streak to earn bonuses.
- 📊 **Progress Dashboard**: See your daily email count, time saved, and streak history.
- 🛑 **Game Off/Game On**: Switch between normal and game mode with a single toggle.
- 🔐 **Privacy-First**: No servers. No tracking. All your data stays in your browser.

## 🏗️ Installation

1. Clone or download this repo.
2. Visit `chrome://extensions/` in your browser.
3. Enable **Developer Mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked**.
5. Select the `src/` folder inside the project (containing manifest.json).

That’s it! Open Gmail and start dashing.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Data

> _"I don’t want your data. I don’t need it, I’m not interested in it, and I’m against the way everyone’s data is always getting exploited."_

This extension has no servers. It does not track, transmit, or store your email data anywhere outside your browser. All game data (like scores and streaks) lives on your device. Your inbox is your business.
